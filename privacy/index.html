DeskShuttle Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-23

DeskShuttle transfers files over your local Wi‑Fi network between your iPhone and a browser on your computer. Transfers do not go through our servers.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not upload your photos, videos, or documents to a cloud service operated by us.
  • We do not require an account.

Data processed on device

  • Photos and files you explicitly select for transfer stay on your devices and local network.
  • Purchase status may be handled by Apple and RevenueCat for Pro unlocks.
